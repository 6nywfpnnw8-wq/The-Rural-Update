In the executed source-selection path, the applicable explicit `ValueError`

determining whether IDs, URLs, or both were duplicated, and prevent an exact
historical replay. This is not evidence that the editorial safeguards are wrong.

## Correction

New source choices are keyed by code-assigned IDs bound to exact observed URLs.
The model supplies only a source title and known publication date, or null to
exclude that source. It cannot generate another ID or URL. Existing canonical
aliases are collapsed before selection. Duplicate JSON fields are rejected
instead of silently overwritten by the JSON decoder.

The unchanged catalog validator still requires 5 to 12 distinct sources. All
existing domain, provenance, reference, date, content, narration, evidence-audit,
audio-completeness and public-delivery gates still apply. Valid earlier stage
caches keep their original identifiers and integrity checks.

Screened visible source completions are saved before validation, explicitly
unapproved, and exported with the attempt ledger in the usual report artifact.
Reports distinguish source selection, malformed JSON, schema failures and duplicate
IDs or URLs without printing source prose or secrets. The inspection workflow
also exports these screened completions. No retry or attempt allowance is added.

The fixed-key nullable metadata schema follows the
[official Structured Outputs requirements](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/structured-outputs).
Local regression tests use synthetic completions. They do not establish a live
provider-schema acceptance or a successful future unattended publication.

## Recovery boundary

No source catalog, complete edition, narration, audit or audio is retained for
October 25. The two daily attempts remain consumed. The discovery notes are
unapproved and cannot be promoted directly into a release. No third attempt,
counter reset, authorization file, publication request or competing clock is
introduced by this correction.

Recovering October 25 would require a separately authorized, one-time continuation
bound to this discovery identity and checksum, with new discovery prohibited and
all normal evidence and delivery gates retained. The ordinary two-attempt policy
and sole Render publication clock remain unchanged. The incident remains open
until actual October 25 public delivery is independently verified.
