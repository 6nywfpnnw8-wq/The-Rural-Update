Track 1 — Executive News (full detail)

National rural OB-access drive-time analysis

Stateline (8/28, citing KFF Medicaid-access data), syndicated 8/30. 14 states where losing the nearest rural hospital/OB unit forces 60+ min drives for Medicaid patients to inpatient maternity care. National median drive time: 43 min rural vs. 13 min urban; obstetric-unit closures alone add ~14 min average travel time (17 min rural vs. 10 min urban). Four closures logged in the two months preceding the report. Distinct from Oklahoma's own already-on-file severity figures (1 of 7 states, ≥90% of rural noncore counties losing OB care 2010–2023).

Early Warning

AI-in-rural-health federal push: HHS Secretary Kennedy and CMS Administrator Oz both publicly framing AI ("AI nurses," behavioral-health avatars) as a rural-access solution, funded partly via RHTP dollars. Oklahoma State University research (via KGOU, 8/19) finds Oklahoma's rural hospitals lagging peers in AI adoption; companion reporting documents patient wariness. Monitor via Track 3 (next runs Friday).
