Executive BLUF

Yesterday's national hospital closure research and Medicare Advantage ratings shift the executive agenda to access and payer accountability. A new JAMA study examines nationwide hospital openings and closures through 2025, while CMS reports 37% of 2027 MA-PD contracts have four or more stars, covering roughly 71% of enrollment. October 15 begins Medicare open enrollment and closes Oklahoma ROOTS Year Two. Oklahoma's RHTP county meetings begin October 26. RHC telehealth production claims, December 31 HIE/EHR applications, January 2027 payer APIs and clinical AI validation remain operational priorities.

Top 5 Developments

1

JAMA charts U.S. hospital closures and service-access consequences

Rural AccessBoard Risk

October 8 research examines American hospital openings and closures during 2010–2025 and travel distance after closures. Reporting describes more than 400 closures, a net loss of 216 hospitals and over 24,000 beds. These are national, not rural-only, figures.

Why it matters: access should be evaluated through actual clinical capabilities, EMS turnaround, transfers and post-acute options, not just a count of hospitals.
Executive question: Which lost services would most increase rural patient travel or delay?
2

CMS publishes 2027 Medicare Advantage and Part D Star Ratings

Medicare AdvantageFinance

On October 8, CMS reported that 37% of 2027 MA-PD contracts have at least four stars, representing about 71% of current MA-PD enrollment. These ratings influence 2028 quality bonus payments. Medicare annual enrollment runs October 15 to December 7.

Why it matters: county-level plan differences affect network adequacy, authorization burden and payment performance. Connect star ratings with denied claims and effective allowed-to-cost ratios.
Executive question: Which local MA contracts deliver the strongest realized reimbursement after administrative friction?
3

RHC telehealth moves into the second week of production review

RHCRevenue Cycle

The October 1 billing conversion is now live. RHC and FQHC distant-site telehealth claims should be moving under individual CPT or HCPCS codes with the applicable telehealth modifiers rather than G2025.

Why it matters: configuration success does not guarantee payment success. The first production week is when payer edits, clearinghouse logic and documentation mismatches become visible.
Executive question: What are the first-week rejection and denial rates by payer, code and modifier?
4

Price-transparency data remains a new contracting tool

Managed CareFinance

The October 5 Transparency in Coverage final rules standardize payer rate files, add utilization and taxonomy context, expand out-of-network data and require accuracy attestations.

Why it matters: standardized payer data can strengthen rural contract analytics when combined with allowed-to-cost ratios, denial burden and network indispensability.
Executive question: Who owns ingestion of the new payer files and how will they be converted into usable negotiating intelligence?
5

Oklahoma RHTP application windows and regional meetings approach

OklahomaRHTP

ROOTS Year Two applications close October 15, while OHCA HIE and EHR assistance applications close December 31. Oklahoma county-collaborative meetings start October 26 and extend into November, including counties served by rural affiliates.

Why it matters: regionally shared capabilities require implementation milestones, specific outcomes and a post-grant operating plan.
Executive question: Which clinical, EMS and technology priorities should each affiliate region advance at its county meeting?
